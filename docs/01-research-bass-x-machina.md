# Research: Bass X Machina

Compiled 10 November 2026, one week after release, from press coverage, reviews and
recaps (listed at the bottom). I have not watched the series, so plot details are
only as good as those write-ups.

## What it is

| | |
|---|---|
| Format | Adult animated limited series, 6 episodes of 29–40 minutes, all released at once |
| Platform / date | Netflix, 3 November 2026 |
| Creator | LeSean Thomas (*Cannon Busters*, *Yasuke*) |
| Studio | Studio Mir (*Devil May Cry*), South Korea / United States co-production |
| Star and executive producer | Brian Tyree Henry, who called it the "first anime Black Western" |
| Music | Theme by Janelle Monáe; score by Roman GianArthur and Nate Wonder; hip-hop over the action scenes |
| Reception | 100% on Rotten Tomatoes from 10 reviews, 82 on Metacritic from 6 |

It is a steampunk Western loosely inspired by Bass Reeves, a real 19th-century lawman
and one of the first Black U.S. Deputy Marshals west of the Mississippi.

## The world

- **Setting:** an alternate America just after the Civil War. Reviews describe a
  "lawless Steampunk West" overrun by outlaws, machines and supernatural threats.
- **Boilertech:** the steam technology that powers this age. Robot bartenders and
  servants are ordinary sights, while racial prejudice is unchanged.
- **Automatons:** boilertech robot sentries. The villain builds military-grade versions
  to sell to warlords.
- **Aianhli:** a mystical, near-indestructible metal sacred to Native peoples. Its
  "cores" power the largest machines, and Bass's arm is made of it.
- **The Holy People:** Choctaw holy women (*Hvshtahli*) who command elements. The show
  never calls them witches. Nanye wields fire and Ahni wields lightning.
- **Prejudice against the augmented:** people with machinery in their bodies face
  extra discrimination.
- **Places:** Central City (the capital), the outlaw town of Prosperity, the deserts
  and canyons of the Badlands, the forests of Choctaw territory, a segregated train,
  and a Tuskegee science fair that doubles as an arms bazaar.

## The characters

| Character | Voice | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Bass Reeves** | Brian Tyree Henry | Former Union soldier turned bounty hunter. A Confederate general experimented on him and replaced his arm with an Aianhli prosthetic that converts into a double-barrelled gun. Cold, masked, fighting to keep his humanity. Rides a horse named Rio. |
| **Dana Starr** | Tati Gabrielle | 14-year-old aspiring engineer, biracial and white-passing, searching for her missing uncle. Rides a motorized scooter. Her gadgets "don't always work." She is the show's conscience. |
| **Ahni** | Cree Summer | The strongest of three holy-woman enforcers. Lightning powers. Nanye's daughter, and so Bass's. |
| **Nanye** | — | Choctaw holy woman with fire powers who saved Bass and loved him. Died defending her clan. |
| **Etta Yellowtail** | Starletta DuPois | Elderly Choctaw engineer and master of Aianhli craft. Rebuilt Bass's arm and mentors Dana. |
| **Lighthorse** | Chaske Spencer | Choctaw leader and Bass's old friend. Allies with the villain to arm his people, then seizes the superweapon himself. |
| **General Rivenbark** | Currie Graham | Sadistic former Confederate general and arms dealer. Made Bass a weapon. |
| **Dr. Eugene Starr** | Nick Thurston | Dana's uncle, an inventor forced to build war machines. |
| **Glory Reeves** | Janelle Monáe | Bass's wife, pregnant through the season. |

Minor antagonists: outlaw **Bully Shaw** and his **Bull** automaton, a trio of
gold-robbing brothers (one with a nine-barrelled machine gun), and Bass's old army
comrades turned train robbers.

## The story in brief

Central City hires Bass to find the missing inventor Eugene Starr, promising to make
him its first Black U.S. Marshal. The trail leads to Rivenbark, who is forcing Eugene
to build Aianhli-core war machines. Dana tags along and keeps challenging Bass's
violence. Ahni is sent to kill Bass and stops when he recognizes her as Nanye's
daughter. At the Tuskegee fair Rivenbark unleashes **Colossus**, a mansion-sized war
machine. Bass spares Rivenbark, Lighthorse hijacks Colossus and turns it on Central
City, and Bass nearly dies destroying its core with his own arm.

## How the action works

- Three kinds of fighter share the screen: gunfighters, people with supernatural
  powers, and people with advanced devices. Reviews single this mix out.
- Bass's style is fast, precise and lethal, built on evasion and his transforming arm.
- Trains are battlegrounds, and the finale is a fight against a building-sized machine.
- Reviewers note the animation steps up whenever the heavy technology arrives.

## Themes

Race, identity, segregation, the Civil War and the Trail of Tears. The central
question is where justice ends and revenge begins. One mid-season episode is described
as "a half-hour debate on the nature of revenge, identity, and survival."

## What this means for WestX

Your three classes map directly onto the show's three kinds of power:

| Your class | The show's version |
|---|---|
| Bounty hunters | Bass |
| Engineers | Dana, Etta, Eugene |
| Witches | The Holy People: Nanye, Ahni and the other enforcers |

**Take the ingredients:**

- The genre blend: Western, steam technology and the supernatural in one world.
- Three power sources in tension, each with its own people.
- Bounty hunting as the structure that moves you through the world.
- Trains as set pieces and giant machines as bosses.
- A modern soundtrack over period visuals.
- A story that is about something.

**Leave the specifics:**

- *Bass X Machina* belongs to Netflix and its creators. WestX cannot use its
  characters, names, designs or plot: no Bass, Dana or Ahni, and no Aianhli,
  boilertech, Colossus or Central City.
- Bass Reeves was a real person, but the show's version of him is theirs.
- The show's mystics are Choctaw holy women, written that way deliberately. WestX's
  witches should practise an invented tradition instead of borrowing a real people's
  sacred one.

The design doc builds an original world on those terms. It is also the version you
could sell.
